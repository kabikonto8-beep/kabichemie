What are we doing?

We measure before recommending.

The cost of water does not end at its intake. The result is also determined by blowdown, water exchange, energy consumption, heat exchange surface status and dosing stability.

Water balance
Collection, blowdown, replacement and amount of water discharged from the installation.
Process efficiency

Operation

From diagnosis to result.

Each stage answers a specific technical question and ends with a clear next step.

  1. We diagnose the system

    We analyze water, load, work history, blowdown and dosing. We determine where the loss or risk arises.

  2. Select the KCAQUA program

    We combine the treatment product, dose, automation and target parameters into one program tailored to working conditions.

  3. We supervise and report

    We follow trends, adjust settings and show the impact of the program on installation parameters and its operating costs.

Decision based on data

Fair diagnosis. Sure decision.

After the analysis, we show what is worth improving, what actions have a rationale and then learn about their effect. If the installation has no real potential, we are talking about it directly.

  • What we measureWater parameters, dosing work, blowdown and loss points.
  • What we recommendAdjust settings, cleaning, audit or KCAQUA program.
  • What do we report?Change of parameters, technical risks and impact on costs.
