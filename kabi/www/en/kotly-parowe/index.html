Audit scope

You don't need complete documentation for your first interview. Missing measurements are arranged together during audit.

    Hardness, pH, conductivity, alkalinity, iron and share of condensate return.

  2. Boiler work

    Pressure, steam production, working hours, load variation and exhaust temperature.

FAQ

Questions about the steam boiler room program.

Specifically about measurement, stopping, blowdown and responsibility.

Where does steam-boiler treatment programme selection begin?

From the analysis of supply water, boiler water and condensate and from the knowledge of pressure, steam production, blowdown and current dosing. Only this image allows you to determine the treatment product, dose and target values.

Does implementation require a boiler-house shutdown?

Analysis, activating dosing and monitoring are usually conducted during normal work. If you need chemical cleaning or changing the components of the installation, the term and the range of stops are determined separately.

How can you tell that scale is already increasing steam costs?

The signal may be increased fuel consumption per unit of steam, higher exhaust temperature, deterioration of heat exchange or sludge visible during the revision. A single symptom is not enough, so we compare trends and test results. We treat

How can blowdown be reduced without compromising safety?

First, we stabilize the quality of the feedwater and dosing, then determine the safe range of conductivity according to the operating conditions and requirements of the boiler manufacturer. We make changes gradually, under measurement control.

What does the plant receive after the audit?

It receives an orderly diagnosis, a list of risks, targets, recommended dosing and monitoring methods and a plan to verify technical and cost effects.
