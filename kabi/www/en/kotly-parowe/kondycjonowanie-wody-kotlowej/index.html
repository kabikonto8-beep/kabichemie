KCAQUA system

The treatment product is an element.
The program creates the result.

KCAQUA combines water analysis, chemical functions, dosing automation and engineering supervision. Each layer of the program is responsible for another part of the stable work of the boiler room.

Identification of water and installations

We combine test results with boiler load, condensate involvement and treatment.

Selection of program functions

We set protection against sediment and corrosion and target values for a particular boiler room.

Work-compatible dosing

The dose can follow the flow of water or change the load, instead of remaining constant.

Control of trends and corrections

We evaluate direction of change, media consumption and the effect of the program, not a single result without context.

FAQ

The most common questions before implementing KCAQUA.

specifically about selection, dosing, measurement and confirmation of the effects of the program.

How does KCAQUA differ from a general-purpose treatment product?

KCAQUA as a program. Includes water and installation recognition, chemical function selection, dose determination, dosing activation and performance control. The treatment product is an important element, but it does not replace the whole process.

How is the treatment-product dose selected?

We take into account the flow of make-up water, the quality of treatment, alkalineity, hardness, boiler conditions, the share of condensate and the expected effect of the program. We confirm the dose by measuring and correcting when started.

How often should the parameters be checked?

At the beginning of the programme, measurements are performed more frequently to stabilise the installation. Later rhythm depends on risk, load variation, automation and repeatability of results.

Can KCAQUA work with existing dosing pumps?

Most often, as long as the pumps have proper performance, adjustment range and compatible materials. Before implementation, we check their condition, injection point and method of control.

How do you verify the programme's results?

We compare trends of water parameters, dosing, blowdown and media consumption, as well as observations from the installation revision. The conclusions are recorded in the report together with recommendations for further corrections.
