Solutions · steam boilers

Chemical demisting of steam boilers

Chemically dissolve the scale in steam boilers, restore heat exchange and protect the installation from failures without costly dismantling.

Set up a free consultation

Service of industrial plants throughout Poland, with technical team in Siedlce and Toruń.

Why descaling the boiler?

The scale acts like an insulator. The thicker the layer, the more fuel it takes to make steam and the greater the risk of overheating and cracking.

Descaling process

  1. 1

    Assessment of state and water

    We determine the type and thickness of sediment.

  2. 2

    Chemical cleaning

    A good treatment product dissolves the scale.

  3. 3

    Rinse and passivation

    We're securing clean surfaces.

  4. 4

    conditioning

    We're implementing the program to keep the scale from coming back.
