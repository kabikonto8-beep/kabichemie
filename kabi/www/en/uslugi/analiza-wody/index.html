Test scope

Parameters are selected for installation.

Otherwise, we read boiler water, or water in the condenser, or else the RO system. The range is determined after talking about installation and symptoms.

  1. Response and alkalinity

    pH and alkalinity describe the direction of corrosion and stability of the chemical program.

  2. Hardness and proneness to sediment

    Assessment of the risk of building scale on heat exchange surfaces.

  3. Conductivity, TDS and chlorides

    Salinity, aggressiveness of water and the basis for setting blowdown and discharges.

  4. Iron and Corrosion Products

    Signal of sludge transport, corrosion of the installation and condensate dirt.

FAQ

Questions about water analysis.

The sample, the scope of the study, the interpretation of the results and what happens after the report.

How should a sample be collected to obtain a reliable result?

The sample shall be taken from a fixed representative point to a clean vessel and preferably during normal operation of the installation. It is important to describe the place of collection, the date and the working conditions, because without this context the number is difficult to reliably assess.

Which parameters are tested?

The scope depends on the system. We typically test pH, alkalinity, hardness, conductivity, chlorides, TDS and iron. For cooling and RO systems, we add indicators related to deposit, corrosion and microbiological risk.

Is a single analysis sufficient?

A single result shows the condition at a given moment and is usually sufficient for a preliminary diagnosis. However, the evaluation of the effectiveness of the chemical program requires a series of measurements, because it is a trend, not a single reading.

What happens after the results are received?

The results are discussed in the context of the operation of the installation and we indicate the next step: correction of dosing, cleaning, passiveness, technical audit or regular monitoring. We don't leave a client with a table of numbers.

Do I need an in-house laboratory or previous test results?

No. If you do not have current measurements, we perform analysis from scratch. If you have results from another source, we can read them and indicate what is missing for the full image.

Do you have the water results up to date?

Send the parameters. We'll help you read them.

You can also order an analysis if you do not have certain data from recent measurements. Just a brief description of the installation and the symptom.
