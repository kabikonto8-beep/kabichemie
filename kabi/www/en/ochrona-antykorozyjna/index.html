Anti-corrosive protection. A program based on cause.

We combine assessment of water, materials and working conditions with proper chemical program, passivation and monitoring. The aim is to ensure stable protection of the installation, which can be confirmed by measurements and observation of trends.

Risk diagnosis

Corrosion symptoms require recognition of the mechanism.

Fertilisers, rust, sediment and rising iron concentrations may result from different conditions. We evaluate oxygen, pH, conductivity, temperature, flow rate, materials and work history before we choose protection.

Oxygen and inappropriate pH

Dissolved oxygen and outside the correct range accelerate the corrosion of steel and the formation of local damage.

Corrosion under sediment

Control points

Measurable parameters of effective protection.

The range of measurements depends on the installation. We select parameters that help evaluate the corrosion mechanism and confirm the protection.

  1. Chemical parameters

    pH, conductivity, hardness, alkalinity, chlorides, sulphates, dissolved oxygen and iron, depending on the system.

  2. Process conditions

    Temperature, pressure, flow, stops, load variation and share of fresh water in circulation.

  3. dosing and automation

    Pump performance, control signals, injection point, alarms, treatment product stock and actual consumption.

  4. Surface status

    Inspections, coupons or corrosive probes, thickness measurements, corrosion products and leak history.

FAQ

Questions about anti-corrosive protection.

Diagnosis, inhibitors, passivation, measurement and evaluation of the effectiveness of the program.

Is a corrosion inhibitor sufficient without prior cleaning?

Not always. If the surface is covered by scale, corrosion or process sediment, the treatment product may not reach the metal evenly. First we evaluate the surface condition and the need to prepare the installation.

How do you determine the cause of corrosion?

We combine water analysis, materials, temperature, flow, damage sites and work history. Depending on the system, we also use viewing, thickness measurement, iron in water, coupons or corrosive probes.

When is passivation required?

Most often after installation, welding, repair or chemical cleaning, when the surface requires the manufacture or restoration of a stable protective layer. The technology is selected for the material and the purpose of the installation.

How often should the protection programme be reviewed?

The frequency depends on the importance of installation, variation of load, quality of additions and stability of automation. At the beginning of the implementation, measurements are usually carried out more frequently and after stabilisation, the operating rhythm is determined.

How can you tell that the programme is working?

We evaluate the trend of water parameters, the concentration of iron, the consumption of the treatment product, the results of surface measurements and the history of the failure. A single sample is not enough for a reliable security confirmation.
