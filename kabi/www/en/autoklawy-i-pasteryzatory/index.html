FAQ

Questions about cleaning autoclaves and process trolleys.

Specifically about the scope of work, the agents used, surface safety and how the service runs.

Does chemical cleaning damage equipment surfaces?

No. We match the agents and process parameters to the type of deposit and the surface material. Contaminants are dissolved chemically, without aggressive mechanical cleaning.

What kinds of soiling do you remove most often?

Burnt-on residue from sterilisation, fats, protein deposits and stubborn technological build-up. We also effectively remove long-standing soiling that standard washing leaves behind.

Can heavily soiled trolleys be renewed instead of replaced?

In many cases, yes. Regeneration restores the cleanliness and functionality of the equipment, and usually costs far less than buying new items.

What does the service process look like?

We start with an audit and assessment of the soiling, select the chemical programme, carry out soaking and deposit removal, and finally check the result and confirm that cleanliness has been restored.

What should you prepare before the first conversation?

The equipment type, the kind of soiling and a short description of the problem are enough. If you have photos of the surfaces, they will speed up the initial assessment of the scope of work.
