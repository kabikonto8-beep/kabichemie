Implementation FAQ

Before cleaning.What is to be determined?

Safe cleaning requires evaluation of the installation, not just the choice of the treatment product.

Does chemical cleaning always require the condenser to be dismantled?

Not always. The possibility of cleaning without dismantling depends on the construction of the installation, the type of sludge, materials and access to circulation. We assess each case before starting work.

How do you protect the system after scale removal?

After cleaning, we select the conditioning program and determine the range of water control. This reduces the conditions that favoured the formation of sediment before intervention.

Can the work be scheduled to minimize its impact on production?

Yes. The scope and schedule are determined after risk assessment and available service windows. In plants with continuous production, it is particularly important to stage and prepare the process in advance.
