Technical FAQ

Job Questionscooling system.

Specific answers help prepare data and move faster from symptom to proper technical decision.

Why does water quality affect evaporative-condenser performance?

The sediment, biofilm and improper salinity limit water contact with the surface of heat exchange. The system can then consume more water and energy and the cooling parameters become less predictable.

Which parameters should be monitored during normal operation?

The range depends on the installation, but usually includes conductivity, pH, hardness of make-up water, alkalinity and microbiological indicators. Equally important are blowdown, water consumption and system load conditions.

Does increasing blowdown always improve system safety?

No. Excessive blowdown increases water consumption and sewage, and still does not solve problems with dosing, water distribution or biofilm. The settings shall result from measurements and balance of the whole system.

When is adjusting the chemical treatment programme alone insufficient?

When the problem arises from obstructive nozzles, uneven spraying, surface damage, incorrect filtration or lack of control over the dosing point. A technical or maintenance correction is also needed.

Where should an assessment of a cooling system begin?

From a short balance: additional water quality, current settings, water consumption, cleaning frequency and observed symptoms. On this basis, measurements can be indicated that will actually help to make a decision.
