FAQ

Questions about the condenser and the cooling circuit.

About water, microbiology, blowdown and implementation without unnecessary abbreviations.

Can one programme control scale, corrosion and biofilm?

These three areas must be shared, but this does not mean one universal dose. The composition of the program, the frequency of the biocide and the range of conductivity are selected for water, materials and system load.

How can water use in an evaporative condenser be reduced?

We analyse concentration cycles, water quality and current blowdown thresholds. Then we stabilize the chemistry and gradually adjust the conductivity to reduce the discharge without increasing the risk of scale or corrosion.

Do you service BAC and EVAPCO ammonia condensers?

Yes. The program is selected for a particular device, materials and working conditions, with particular emphasis on coils, galvanized surfaces, water quality and manufacturer's requirements.

How do you control microbiological risk?

We combine proper biocidal program with regular control, hygiene of the device and documentation of results. The scope is determined taking into account the obligations of the installation owner and risk assessment for the plant.

Does implementation require the cooling system to be stopped?

Analysis, correction of dosing and most of the automation work can be performed during operation. Cleaning, repairs or activities requiring access to the interior of the device are planned in the agreed service window.
