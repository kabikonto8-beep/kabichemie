FAQ

Questions about performance and protection of RO membranes.

Specific answers about diagnosis, anti-scalant, monitoring and the right time of CIP washing.

How can scaling be distinguished from biofouling?

We compare the difference between pressures, normal flow and salt retention with water analysis, work history and sediment from filters or CIP. The conductivity of permeate itself does not allow to reliably indicate the cause.

Can an antiscalant resolve every cause of reduced performance?

No. Antiscalant reduces the risk of precipitation of certain salts. It will not replace proper pre-filtering, biological control, oxidizer protection or proper membrane washing.

How is the antiscalant dose selected?

Based on the ion composition of water, pH, temperature, assumed recovery and concentrate conditions. Selection should also take into account the limitations of the membrane manufacturer and the actual stability of dosing.

Which parameters should be monitored daily?

Flows, pressures, pressure difference, conductivity permeate and concentrate, recovery and temperature. To assess the condition of the membranes of the value, normalize and analyse as a trend.

When should CIP cleaning be performed?

The decision is based on a change in normal flow, salt retention and pressure differential and on recommendations of the membrane manufacturer. Too late washing can fix the sediment, and too often overburdens membranes and production.
