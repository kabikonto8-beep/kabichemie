QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

We have collected answers to the most common questions about technology, implementation and possible savings. However, we know that every installation works differently and that it is impossible to reliably assess all costs, risks and effects without knowing the parameters of the plant.

Therefore, we encourage you to contact us directly or to arrange a free audit. We will identify areas of loss, estimate the potential for savings and propose concrete next steps.

Ask us about the installation ↗
How does KCAQUA technology differ from conventional boiler-water treatment?

KCAQUA is based on the original polymer and phosphonate based technology, which allows to achieve higher performance of the installation while reducing water and energy consumption. In many cases, it reduces the blowdown frequency.

Does KCAQUA replace the treatment chemicals currently in use?

Yes. The KCAQUA chemical treatment programme can replace existing solutions used in steam boilers, cooling systems and selected industrial systems.

How much can KCAQUA technology save?

This depends on the type of installation, water quality and the chemical programme currently used. In selected cases, water and energy savings reach up to 70%.

Why does saving water also save energy?

Each liter of hot water removed from the installation means the loss of energy. Reduce water discharges and exchanges also reduces the energy needed to heat or cool the system.

Is the technical consultation free of charge?

Yes. The first consultation and initial analysis of the potential for savings are free of charge and do not require cooperation.
